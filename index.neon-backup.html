About CloseCraft

Most sites look fine.
They just don't
do anything.

Whether you're a tradie, a DJ, a fashion brand, or a community publication — your site is either working for you or costing you. There's no middle ground. Visitors show up, form an impression in seconds, and leave or stay based on what they find.

CloseCraft builds sites that hold attention. Clean, fast, built around what your audience actually needs — not what looks impressive in a pitch deck.

"A polished site isn't a luxury. It's the difference between looking credible and getting ignored."

Slow or generic design
53% of visitors leave if a page takes more than 3 seconds. Generic templates don't convert — people can tell immediately.
Not built for mobile
Most people will see your site on a phone first. If it's clunky on mobile, they're gone before they read a word.
No clear next step
If your site doesn't make it obvious what to do next — book, call, buy, subscribe — you're losing people who would have converted.

The Story

Why CloseCraft exists

CloseCraft started from a straightforward observation: a lot of good businesses had terrible websites. Not because they didn't care, but because building a proper site felt expensive, complicated, or like something they'd get to eventually.

The brief has expanded since then. Today CloseCraft builds sites for property maintenance companies, luxury fashion brands, local newspapers, DJ studios, and journalists — whatever the project, the approach stays the same.

Understand the brief properly. Build something that fits. Make it fast, polished, and theirs. No jargon, no lock-in, no padding the scope.
