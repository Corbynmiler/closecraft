03 — Process

Simple. Fast.
Done.

Three steps, roughly two weeks. No mystery, no jargon, no fifteen-tab onboarding deck. You'll know what you're getting before the first invoice.

i.

A 15-minute call.

No pressure. We look at what you've got, talk about what you need, and give straight feedback. You'll leave with a clear sense of the work and the cost.

ii.

We build the site.

Design, copy, mobile layout, fast hosting — handled. You stay focused on running your business; we send drafts as we go. Two rounds of revisions baked in.

iii.

Live on your domain.

It launches on your own domain and you own it outright. Enquiries start coming in. Need a tweak down the track? We make it easy.

04 — Why CloseCraft exists

A lot of good businesses
had terrible websites.

Not because they didn't care — but because building a proper one felt expensive, complicated, or like something they'd eventually get around to. CloseCraft started from that gap.

These days the brief covers everything from property maintenance to luxury fashion to community newspapers. The approach stays the same: understand the work properly, build something that fits, make it fast and polished and theirs. No padding, no lock-in.
